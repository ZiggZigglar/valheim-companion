Valheim Companion

Updated for Valheim 1.0 (released Sept 9, 2026). Every number here was checked against the Valheim wiki (valheim.fandom.com / valheim.weirdgloop.org) and the official 1.0 patch notes in September 2026. Weapon damage and armor are quality 1; shields show block armor at skill 0. An item's biome is the earliest one where you can get everything it needs. Deep North coverage is thin because the wikis are still catching up: recipes and enemy resistances that aren't published yet have been left out rather than guessed. Arrows without a listed recipe haven't been re-verified. Click a biome in the bar above to filter; click it again to clear.
